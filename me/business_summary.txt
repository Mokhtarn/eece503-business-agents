Keys & Strings Piano & Guitar Shop is a fictional independent music retailer based in Beirut, Lebanon. The shop focuses on acoustic and digital pianos, acoustic and electric guitars, and electric-guitar amplifiers. It also carries a practical range of accessories, including strings, picks, cables, tuners, cases, pedals, benches, stands and headphones. By concentrating on pianos and guitars instead of selling every type of instrument, the store aims to give customers informed, straightforward advice.

The store serves beginners, students, parents, hobbyists, teachers and performing musicians. Its team includes Mokhtar Nasser, co-founder and piano specialist; Muhiddine Aleily, co-founder and guitar specialist; and Jad Haidar, who handles customer experience and operations. Keys & Strings offers guided instrument selection, showroom demonstrations, basic guitar restringing and setup by appointment, introductory advice on digital-piano connectivity and guitar-amp use, delivery coordination, and after-sales support. The Beirut showroom is open Monday through Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and is closed on Sundays. Eligible boxed-item delivery is listed at $10 within Beirut and $20 within Mount Lebanon, subject to staff confirmation; other areas and acoustic-piano moves require a separate quote.

The shop's main promise is to recommend instruments that match each customer's budget, musical goals, experience and living situation rather than pushing the most expensive option. Unused standard-stock items may be requested for return within 14 calendar days of receipt with original packaging and proof of purchase, subject to inspection. Opened consumables, acoustic pianos, and special orders have separate terms; approved refunds are generally processed within 10 business days after inspection. Warranty terms differ by product. Prices are fictional USD store figures, stock is simulated, and special-order prices are indicative. Product models, specifications, prices and stock must be checked against the approved catalogue, which will be maintained separately. The chatbot can answer general questions, collect contact details from interested customers, and log unanswered enquiries, but it must not invent product facts, accept payment, or promise a reservation.
